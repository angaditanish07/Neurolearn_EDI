How to use NeuroLearn

  1. Register as a student to get your family link code.
  2. Complete Interactive Learning and Dyslexia Screening.
  3. Parents register separately and enter the link code on the Parent Dashboard.

Talk to a teacher

Contact your school educator for classroom support. NeuroLearn supplements — does not replace professional assessment.

Parent dashboard

Parents can view linked children's progress, screening recommendations, and request video check-ins.
